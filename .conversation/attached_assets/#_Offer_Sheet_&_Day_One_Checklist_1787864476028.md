\# PART 1 — OFFER SHEET

\#\#\# 1\. What I do  
I make hire-me websites for trade workers.

\#\#\# 2\. Who it's for  
It's for trade workers in San Diego who have a good reputation but no online  
presence for customers to reach out.

\#\#\# 3\. Price  
I will be selling my websites for $150 for a one-page website. Adults who sell  
websites to small businesses often offer highly expensive websites that are  
$500–$1,000. DIY site builders like Wix or Squarespace cost $15–20 a month,  
which is over $200 every year, and the trade worker still has to build it  
himself.

My price is low right now because I'm just starting and I don't have live sites  
to show yet. My first clients get an introductory price while I build my  
portfolio, and the price goes up once I have finished sites and reviews to show.

\*(Note: the client also pays about $12/year for their own domain name. That is  
their cost, not mine, because they need to own their own web address.)\*

\#\#\# 4\. What's included and what isn't  
1\. One-page hire-me site that sends new clients straight to their phone.  
2\. Includes 2 rounds of changes before launch, and 30 days of free bug fixes  
   after launch.  
3\. I charge extra for more changes than the ones included and for more pages.  
   I will never be the middleman between the clients and the owner of the website.

\#\#\# 5\. How they reach me and how they pay me  
\*\*Reach me:\*\* my business Gmail (making it this week) or call/text my phone.  
\*\*Pay me:\*\* through my mom's Venmo account, which she passes to me. Already  
tested and working.

\#\#\# 6\. What I need before I can start  
\*\*Owned:\*\* MacBook, personal phone, AI subscriptions and tools (Claude, Replit).

\*\*Needed (all $0):\*\* business Gmail, Netlify account and learning how to use it,  
Web3Forms account, and from each client — their photos, phone number, services,  
and license status.

The client pays for their own domain (\~$12/year). I help them set it up but I  
don't buy it.

\*\*Total new money I need: $0\*\* (cap was $20)

\#\#\# 7\. Rejection sentence  
"Alright, I understand — thanks for your time. If you ever meet clients who want  
to see your work beforehand, or if work slows down, you can find my info on my  
dad's website."
