# Future plans

Direction, not a backlog. Nothing here is scheduled. Trade Sites SD ships first;
this is where it grows toward.

## Parent agency brand

Trade Sites SD solves one problem for one audience: a free-agent trade worker
with a good reputation and nowhere to send a customer. That focus is the reason
it works, and it is deliberately narrow.

The parent brand is the wider version of the same business: full custom web
development for small businesses, not one-page hire-me sites off a template.
Trade Sites SD becomes one niche service under it rather than the whole company
— the proof that the approach works, kept intact rather than absorbed.

What that implies:

- The agency is its own site and its own repo. Trade Sites SD keeps its domain,
  its voice, and its $150 entry offer.
- Client work moves from a fixed one-page product to scoped custom projects,
  with pricing to match.
- The audience shifts from "someone who hears about you on a job site" to small
  business owners who already know they need a site and are choosing who builds
  it. Different decision, different length of consideration, different page.
- Trade Sites SD stays the honest storefront: a real niche, real clients, real
  results, linked from the agency as evidence rather than marketing copy.

## Spec and feature goals

The current site is deliberately plain: no build step, one hero animation, two
scroll reveals. That is correct for a trade worker on LTE. The agency site is a
portfolio for web development itself, so the work has to demonstrate range.

- **High-end UI/UX.** The craft is the pitch. Considered type, real layout
  systems, states that feel deliberate. The bar is work a prospect would want
  for themselves.
- **Advanced animation.** Beyond one load sequence — orchestrated transitions,
  scroll-linked motion, interactive components that reward attention. Still
  under `prefers-reduced-motion`, still readable without JavaScript. The
  progressive-enhancement rule does not relax as the ambition rises.
- **Rich media.** Video, real photography, case-study captures. This is the
  first thing the current site cannot do: `images/` holds one favicon and every
  graphic is CSS. An agency site needs an asset pipeline, which likely means
  accepting a build step for the first time.
- **Testimonials.** Named clients with real quotes and real outcomes. Governed
  by the existing rule: nothing invented, nothing published before it is true.
  Empty states stay honest until there is something to fill them with.
- **Niche service branches.** Trade Sites SD is the first. The pattern is a
  focused offer for a specific trade or profession, each with its own landing
  page, each linked from the agency, each able to stand alone.
