About

Built in San Diego. Son of the trades.

Trade Sites SD started with a practical problem in Kevin’s own family: Noe had twenty years of reputation, but nowhere simple to send a new customer who wanted to see his work.

So Kevin built the first site for his dad. Not as a grand agency launch — as a useful tool for a real worker. That is still the standard: make the work easy to understand, make the phone easy to find, and keep the price honest.
