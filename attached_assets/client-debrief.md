## What you get

A one-page website that shows your best work and lets customers call or text you
straight from it. Built for phones, because that's where your customers are.

On it: your photos, your services, reviews from past customers, your service
area, and a call button that's always one tap away.

## What it costs

**$150.** One time, not monthly.

Plus about **$12 a year** for your domain name — your web address. You buy that
in your own name, so you own it.

Hosting is free. There is no monthly fee unless you want one.

**Compare:** a freelancer charges $500 or more for the same one page. An agency
charges trade businesses thousands. Building it yourself on Wix or Squarespace
costs over $200 every year, forever — and you're the one building it.

## How we do it

1. **You text us.** Tell us your trade and your service area.
2. **You send photos and info.** We'll send you a guide on exactly what to take.
3. **We build it.** About two weeks from the day we have your photos.
4. **You look it over.** Two rounds of changes are included.
5. **It goes live**, and you pay the rest.

**Payment:** half up front, half when you approve it.

## After it's live

- **30 days of free fixes.** If anything breaks, we fix it, no charge.
- After that, changes are **$25 each**.
- Or **$10 a month** for up to 2 small updates a month — new photos, a new
  service, a phone number change — plus we keep an eye on the site and remind
  you before your domain renews.

The monthly plan is optional. Most clients don't need it.

## What you own

Everything.

- **The domain is in your name.** You pay for it, you control it.
- **The site is yours** once it's paid for.
- **Your customers contact you directly.** Calls, texts, and form messages go
  straight to your phone. We never see them and we don't manage your leads.

If you ever want to leave, you take the whole thing with you. We don't hold
anything hostage.

## Languages

We build in English, Spanish, or both.
